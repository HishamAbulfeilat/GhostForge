# Agent World — recommendations

Features and bigger optimizations for Agent World that were **not** done in
the October 2026 optimization pass. Ranked by user value against effort.
Effort: **S** (under half a day), **M** (one to two days), **L** (more).

"Shared" means `web-ui/app/agent-world/shared/` here and
`src/agent-world/shared/` in the external Agent World app. Those two folders are
byte-identical (see the external repo's `SHARED.md`), so change both in the same
way.

Already done in that pass (both repos unless noted): stable character order
(no more slot, sprite or desk reshuffles), memoized merged world data
(GhostForge page), town bubble layout skipped while nothing moves, an immediate
refresh when the tab becomes visible again, and phone-width fixes for the
minimap and the AI Town toolbar.

| # | What | User value | Effort | Where |
|---|------|-----------|--------|-------|
| 1 | **Pause scenes that are off screen.** The PixiJS stage (AI Town) and the Phaser game (Agent Office) render at 60 fps while the user scrolls down to the status columns, the activity log or the session drawer. Add an `IntersectionObserver` on the scene host and stop the Pixi ticker or `game.loop.sleep()` while it is not visible. Also stop the bubble and minimap `requestAnimationFrame` loops. | Battery and CPU on laptops and phones. This is the largest remaining rendering cost. | S–M | Shared `town/TownStage.tsx`, `office/OfficeStage.tsx`, `Minimap.tsx`. The Pixi app is reached through the vendored `Game`; record any change in `vendor/ai-town/NOTICE.md` in both repos. |
| 2 | **Skip unchanged snapshots.** `useCliWorld` replaces `world` on every poll, even when nothing changed. Every memo (`worldAgents`, adapters, the town iframe `postMessage`, `office.update`) then re-runs every 5 to 10 s. Compare a cheap key, such as each session's id, `updatedAt`, status and context, and keep the previous object when it is equal. The heartbeat must stay live for the "updated Ns ago" label. | Fewer re-renders and fewer scene updates with idle sessions, which is the common case. | S | Shared `api.ts` |
| 3 | **Hash-based slots and sprites.** Characters still get their town slot, sprite and office desk by list index, so a session that leaves the live set moves everyone after it. Use a stable hash of the session id for the sprite and the first free slot for placement, and keep a slot until its session leaves. | Characters keep their place and look across refreshes and when you switch between Live and Today. | M | `town/agent-town-model.js` and `office/agent-office-model.js` in both repos. Keep both adapters in sync. |
| 4 | **Cache AI Town spritesheets and free them on unmount.** The vendored `Character.tsx` builds and parses a new `Spritesheet` for every character instance and never destroys it. Textures pile up as sessions come and go, and when the town view mounts again. Share one parsed sheet per `textureUrl` (with reference counting) and destroy the textures when the last user goes. | Lower memory over long sessions. Faster town mounts. | M | `vendor/ai-town/src/components/Character.tsx` in both repos. Record it as a deviation in `NOTICE.md`. |
| 5 | **Push instead of polling.** Serve `/api/world` (external) and `/api/agents/cli-sessions` (GhostForge) as Server-Sent Events from the collector's file watcher. Keep polling as the fallback. | Needs-you alerts arrive within a second instead of 5 to 10 s. Less collector work when nothing changes. | M–L | `server.mjs` and `collector.mjs` (external); `web-ui/lib/cli-sessions.mjs` and the route (GhostForge); shared `api.ts` |
| 6 | **Touch controls for Agent Office.** The office pans with the arrow keys or WASD only. On a phone or tablet the only way to move is the minimap. Add drag-to-pan and pinch-to-zoom on the Phaser camera. Manual panning must clear the follow target, as the minimap `pan()` already does. | Agent Office works on phones and tablets. | M | Shared `office/OfficeStage.tsx`, or the vendored `Game.ts` with a `NOTICE.md` note |
| 7 | **Check shared-code drift in CI.** Nothing fails when the two copies of `shared/` or `vendor/` diverge. One option is to commit a manifest of SHA-256 hashes for `web-ui/app/agent-world/shared/**` and check it in `npm test`; a sync PR then updates both repos and the manifest together. The other is a scheduled job that runs the `diff -r` from `SHARED.md` against a checkout of the external repo. | Prevents silent forks between the two apps. | S | `web-ui/test/` (new test) and the external repo's `test/` |
| 8 | **External app: stop the once-a-second re-render of the whole page.** `useNow()` sits at the top of `App.tsx`, so every world (and the Pixi or Phaser host component) re-renders every second, and the inline `onSelect` closures defeat memoization. Move the clock into the header's "updated Ns ago" label and the dashboard. Pass `useCallback` handlers to the worlds. The GhostForge page needs the same treatment for its other children. | Smoother scenes on slower machines, and less garbage-collection churn. | S | External `src/App.tsx`, `src/worlds/*/*.tsx`; GhostForge `web-ui/app/agent-world/page.tsx` |
| 9 | **Chat with Copilot CLI sessions.** The chat box handles Claude Code sessions only. Copilot CLI has a non-interactive mode, so the same stdin/no-shell pattern should work. | Feature parity for both providers. | M | Shared `server/chat.mjs` and `ChatBox.tsx`, plus both chat routes |
| 10 | **Session timeline replay.** The collector already keeps a per-session `timeline` and events. Add a scrubber in the session drawer that replays the tool, subagent and compaction events, and moves the character in the scene to match. | Shows what a long-running agent actually did while you were away. | L | Shared `SessionDrawer.tsx` and `world-model.ts`, plus both collectors |
| 11 | **Warm the world bundles on intent.** Phaser (about 1.5 MB) and the AI Town bundle (about 0.7 MB) load only when their tab opens. Start the dynamic import on hover or focus of the world switcher button. | The first switch to a world feels instant. | S | `web-ui/app/agent-world/AgentWorldSwitcher.tsx`; external `src/App.tsx` tabs |
| 12 | **A text alternative per scene.** The canvases are `role="img"` with a fixed label. Generate an `aria-label` summary ("3 working, 1 needs you: forge-api waiting for you"). Make the minimap reachable by keyboard: arrow keys pan, Enter focuses the selected character. | Screen-reader and keyboard users get the state of the scene, not only the columns below it. | S | Shared `town/TownStage.tsx`, `office/OfficeStage.tsx`, `Minimap.tsx` |
